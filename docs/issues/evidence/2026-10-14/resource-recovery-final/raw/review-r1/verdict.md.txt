**F1 — Startup deadline and caller cancellation bypass the owned asynchronous cancellation path.**
Location: [Tt.cs:107](/home/dan/code/TurboTest/src/TurboTest.EFCore/Tt.cs:107), `InitializeOwnedPoolAsync`. A timed source is directly linked to the token passed to initialization and used by `WaitAsync`. The later `linked.CancelAsync()` does not own cancellation already initiated by that timer or the caller.

This produces three related failures:

- A throwing seed cancellation callback can propagate through the linked source onto the deadline timer thread, escaping the lifecycle error handler and potentially terminating the test process.
- A blocking callback registered after `WaitAsync` can delay that observer’s cancellation notification. If initialization awaits a noncooperative task, the configured startup deadline need not return control to the caller.
- The catch-path `CancelAsync()` does not join callbacks already running; initialization can settle and resource disposal can start before those callbacks finish.

These conclusions follow from the candidate call chain and .NET 10’s implementation: timed and linked cancellation invoke handlers synchronously, and `CancelAsync()` returns immediately when cancellation has already been requested. [.NET 10 CancellationTokenSource source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dotnet/runtime/v10.0.0/src/libraries/System.Private.CoreLib/src/System/Threading/CancellationTokenSource.cs)

The existing tests miss this combination. `Throwing_startup_cancellation_callbacks_cannot_replace_the_initializer_error_or_lose_cleanup` initiates cancellation through the initializer-failure catch path. The deadline variant of `A_blocking_startup_cancellation_callback_keeps_its_host_owned_without_blocking_the_failure_observer` returns a token-cancelable `Task.Delay`, which supplies another route to initialization completion; its callback does not throw.

**Required outcome:** Separate caller/deadline observation from cancellation of initialization work. Ensure every cancellation origin starts and retains one asynchronous cancellation task; bound the observer independently, capture callback failures safely, and join cancellation plus initialization before resource disposal. Add behavioral RED/GREEN evidence for deadline and caller cancellation with a noncooperative initializer and a held callback, including a late callback failure. Assert bounded caller completion, exact resource retention until work settles, safe error reporting, and eventual single cleanup. A deadline-triggered throwing-callback RED should be isolated from the main test runner.
